Microfluidics

Chips that were impossible to print

Microfluidic chips demand two things SLA could never deliver together: extremely fine channels and optical clarity. PDMS soft lithography was the only path. Not anymore.

The old way: PDMS

  • Cleanroom master molds
  • Manual casting and bonding
  • Days per design iteration
  • Limited geometry freedom
→

The Aionik way: SLA

  • Print directly, no molds
  • Ultra-fine, crystal-clear channels
  • Iterate designs in hours
  • Full 3D channel geometry
